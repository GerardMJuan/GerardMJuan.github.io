## About Me

I'm Gerard Martí Juan, a **Senior AI Researcher** at [Eden](https://eden.ai) and **Research Scientist** at [Universitat Pompeu Fabra - BCN Medtech](https://www.upf.edu/web/bcn-medtech), based in Barcelona, Spain.

My work focuses on developing advanced AI and machine learning solutions for medical imaging, with particular expertise in brain MRI analysis, neuroimaging, and healthcare AI applications. I have over 4 years of postdoctoral experience building end-to-end ML pipelines, deep learning models, and clinical decision support systems.

### Background

I completed my **Ph.D. in Information and Communication Technologies** at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in 2021 (Cum Laude), where my thesis focused on data-driven methods to characterize heterogeneity in Alzheimer's disease. Prior to that, I obtained an MSc in Computer Vision from Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and a Bachelor's degree in Informatics Engineering from Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya.

### Research Interests

- Medical image analysis and computer-aided diagnosis
- Deep learning for neuroimaging (MRI, fMRI, diffusion MRI)
- Disease progression modeling and predictive analytics
- Multimodal data integration in healthcare
- AI for radiology and clinical decision support
